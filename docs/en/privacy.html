Privacy Policy

Tilecheck (iPhone app) / Last updated September 22, 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy日本語

Information we collect

None. This app does not collect any information about you. It does not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or usage data.

Data you enter

The items you write, their check states, and settings such as colors are stored only on your iPhone. No one else, including the developer, can see them.

Deleting the app deletes this data too.

Network

The app connects to the network only for the optional “Buy the developer a coffee” in Settings (an in-app purchase). It contacts Apple’s App Store to show the price and to process the tip. It does not connect to the internet otherwise. Your items are never sent to a server, and no external services are used.

Microphone and speech recognition

Only while “Read aloud” is on, the app uses the microphone to hear answers such as “got it” or “next”. Recognition happens entirely on your iPhone. Your voice and the recognized words are never sent anywhere and are not stored. Read aloud also works without this permission (answer with the on-screen buttons).

In-app purchase

“Buy the developer a coffee” is a voluntary tip to the developer. Apple handles the payment; the developer never receives your card details or Apple Account. Tipping does not change anything in the app.

Sharing with third parties

Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to share. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Accounts

No account or sign-in is required.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date at the top.
