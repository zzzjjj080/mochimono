How to use

Write your items

Open a list and tap “Edit” to write it as text. One item per line.

Wallet
Phone
Keys

Charger
Earbuds

A blank line starts a new group with its own color. In the example above, “Wallet, Phone, Keys” and “Charger, Earbuds” get different colors. Groups have no names; they are told apart by color only.

Get ready

Tap an item when it’s done and its color deepens. Tap again to undo. The bar above the board shows how many are done, like “3 / 10”, and grows as you go.

When everything is done, the bar turns green and says “All set!” Tap it to clear all checks and start fresh for next time.

Clear the checks

Checks stay until you tap “Clear all” at the bottom of the board. If you close the app halfway through, you can pick up where you left off.

Add, duplicate and delete lists

Tap “+” on the first screen to add a list. You can start from a template (travel, commute, camping and more), from blank, or by pasting text from another app.

Swipe a list right to duplicate it, or left to delete it. Deleting always asks first.

Change colors

Below the board you’ll see ◀ color swatches ▶. The arrows step through 10 color schemes, and the swatches show the colors. Each list keeps its own colors.

Turn “Color” off to show every group in one color. Turn it on again to bring the groups back.

Columns and appearance

Open a list and tap “Settings”. Columns (2 to 5) are set per list. Light / Dark / Automatic applies to the whole app.

Share as text

In “Settings”, tap “Export as text”. The board is shared as ordinary text with no special format. The person who receives it pastes it into “Add list” and gets the same board.

FAQ

Why don’t checks clear by themselves?

So you can close the app or switch to something else and come back without losing your place. If checks disappeared on their own, you wouldn’t know how far you got.

Do checks disappear when I edit?

No. Items that keep the same name keep their checks, even if you reorder them or move them to another group. Only items you delete lose their check.

Can I name the groups?

Not at the moment. Groups are told apart by color only.

Which languages are supported?

English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian and Arabic. The app follows your iPhone’s language; other languages show in English.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Nothing you write is sent to the internet. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you restore your new iPhone from a backup. There is no separate transfer feature in the app.
