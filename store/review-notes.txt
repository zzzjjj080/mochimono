WHAT THE APP DOES
Tilecheck is a reusable checklist. The user writes the items as plain text, one
per line. A blank line starts a new group, and each group is automatically given
a different colour. The items are shown as a grid of tiles; tapping one marks it
as done (the tile fills with a stronger colour). Checks stay until the user
clears them, so the same list can be used again and again - packing for a trip,
the morning commute, a shopping run, or a pre-departure safety check.

The bundle identifier is com.zzzjjj080.Mochimono. This was registered before the
app was named, and is intentionally left unchanged; it is internal only and is
not shown to users.

HOW TO EXERCISE THE APP
1. On launch, three lists are already present (Town / Commute / Domestic trip).
   Tap any of them.
2. Tap any tile. It turns a stronger colour and the counter at the top right
   increases. Tap again to undo.
3. Tap "全部外す" (Clear all) at the bottom left. A confirmation alert appears
   stating how many checks will be removed. This is the only destructive action
   on the check state.
4. Tap "編集" (Edit) at the bottom right to see the plain-text editor. Adding a
   blank line splits the items into another colour group. Existing checks are
   preserved by item name when the text is edited.
5. Tap "設定" (Settings) in the navigation bar to choose one of eight colour
   schemes for that list, the number of columns (2-5), and light/dark appearance
   for the whole app.
6. Tap "+" on the first screen to add a list. Twelve templates are offered
   (travel, commute, gym, camping, disaster kit, and so on). Choosing one copies
   it into an editable list. A blank list can also be created.
7. Swipe a row left on the first screen to delete a list. A confirmation alert
   appears naming the list and how many items will be lost.

DATA AND PRIVACY
All data is stored on the device using UserDefaults. Nothing is transmitted.
The app does not use any third-party SDK, analytics, or advertising.

External services: none. Only Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, UIKit for haptics).
Regional differences: none. The app is Japanese only and is distributed in Japan.
The app name is in Latin script but the interface is entirely in Japanese.
Not a medical app. No diagnosis, treatment, or health claims.
Accessibility: text scales down to fit tiles; colour contrast between the tile
fill and its label is verified to meet WCAG AA (4.5:1) by automated tests for
every colour scheme in both light and dark appearance.
